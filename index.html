Research Interests

XR that works for everyone in the room

I research multi-user XR and asymmetric collaboration: CAVE, head-mounted display, mobile phone and desktop users sharing one experience and each contributing fully. When people can join with the device they already have, skills transfer faster and more people can take part. That is the vision behind my work at RWTH Aachen.

I build the systems I study. The themes below are what I am working on, and where I am looking for partners.

Locomotion & Presence

How movement models in multi-user VR - across headset, desktop and CAVE clients - shape comfort, presence and collaboration.

Gaze & Eye Tracking

Using eye-tracking data for gaze-based interaction, attention analysis and world-space selection in immersive environments.

Mixed Reality & Passthrough

Blending the real desk, keyboard and screen into virtual workspaces so people can work naturally inside XR.

Avatars & Embodiment

Body, hand and face tracking for believable avatars, and how embodiment changes the way people behave in shared spaces.

Asymmetric Collaboration

CAVE, head-mounted display, mobile and desktop users in one shared session, each with a role that fits their device and all working toward the same goal.

Inclusive & Accessible XR

Designing immersive technology and creative careers that are open to people of all abilities.

VR Store Platform (Kadence International)

About

Kadence innovates by choosing Virtual World as a medium for conducting the platform for research. The platform shows marketplace research conclusions on customers inexpensively without additional costs.

The Challenge

Analyzing customer behavior and creating any scenario in the store using VR platform technology. This platform is useful for conducting customer behavior research without spending a lot of investment and time.

The Solution

A complete Virtual Reality Platform for analyzing customer behavior in the mini markets and supermarket stores. The researcher can make their scenario to create shelves and put products. After that researcher can analyze the result using a heat map and also ‘time to pick up’ calculation. Core features: Movement and Eye-Tracking, Virtual Token/Money, Virtual Shopping Experience, Adjustable Product Positioning and Scenario, Heatmap and Data Analytics.

My Contributions

1. Co-Develop using Unreal Engine

2. Develop the heatmap system and capturing the experience data (gaze, navigation, point of interest, and other timing data)

3. Design UI/UX

Results

Achieve 76.62% cost efficiency and 33.81% profit optimizations using VR solution rather than traditional ones.

Codename: mindVoke

About

Codename: mindVoke is more than just a shooter. It is an immersive experience that combines intense gunplay with stunning post-modern surrealist art. We are committed to pushing the boundaries of VR technology to create a game that truly allows players to explore the limits of their imagination.

The Challenge: Building a Thrilling and Accessible Experience

We're creating a fast-paced and strategic battle experience that keeps players hooked. Here's what we're focusing on:

- Intense, Streamlined Gameplay: We want players to focus on the action, not a cluttered interface. We'll use a clear and simple user interface (UI) with easy-to-understand controls and information.

- Smooth and Responsive Gameplay: Smooth gameplay is key! We'll optimize the connection for a lag-free experience, even with a high number of players.

- Easy to Learn, Hard to Master Weapons: Our weapons will be intuitive and easy to pick up, but offer depth and mastery for dedicated players.

Why this matters?

Many arcade-style games struggle with player retention. We want to create a game that people get hooked on, keep coming back to, and enjoy mastering. By focusing on these challenges, we'll deliver a truly engaging experience for players of all skill levels.

SpaceCollab

About

SpaceCollab is an immersive collaboration platform that provides virtual spaces for remote training and collaborations.

The Challenge

The global pandemic has restricted our ability to connect emotionally and share knowledge due to limitations on in-person gatherings. Additionally, training can be expensive as it requires resources for space, equipment, and other costs for both trainers and participants. Moreover, access to rooms, machines, and tools necessary for human development is often limited, further hindering opportunities for growth and learning.

The Solution

We offer SpaceCollab, a virtual collaboration platform with several key features. Firstly, it enables multiplayer interaction, allowing multiple individuals to inhabit the same virtual space and engage with one another. Secondly, SpaceCollab is multiplatform, supporting various VR and non-VR devices, making it accessible to anyone. Thirdly, it provides true immersive presence through customizable avatars and spatial audio. Fourthly, it fosters interactivity by enabling users to engage with objects and other players within the virtual environment. Lastly, SpaceCollab is highly customizable, allowing users to easily tailor scenarios and incorporate additional objects according to their needs.

My Contributions

1. Set the product vision, because developing VR requires many human resources and time-consuming. Based on our previous experience in doing many B2B business. Need customizable VR and modular

2. Leading the team

3. Designing and developing the cloud system, because at that time we cannot hire backend developers because of the resource scarcity

4. Solving multiplatform (VR, Mobile, Desktop), designing the interactions that fits to all platform.

Results

Through the year and COVID Pandemic situation, SpaceCollab has reduced the development time, previously 3 months project to 1 month project. Maintaining effectiveness of the development lifecycle and boost B2B sales of the company.
