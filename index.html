About Me

Who am I?

I'm Andrew Puika, an XR researcher and developer who turns ideas into working immersive systems

I build multi-user VR and mixed-reality applications with Unreal Engine 5 (C++ and Blueprint) for research, education and industry - from headset and desktop clients to CAVE/nDisplay installations. Over the years I have led product and engineering teams, shipped platforms such as Millealab, and taught the next generation of developers.

Today I work on XR research in Germany and help build an inclusive talent ecosystem as COO of LAYAK Foundation. Catalize is where I share my work: if you have a project, a study or a research idea that needs a builder, I would love to hear from you.

What I am curious about

Research Interests

My research sits where interaction design meets engineering: I build the systems I study. The themes below are what I am working on and looking for partners in.

Locomotion & Presence

How movement models in multi-user VR - across headset, desktop and CAVE clients - shape comfort, presence and collaboration.

Gaze & Eye Tracking

Using eye-tracking data for gaze-based interaction, attention analysis and world-space selection in immersive environments.

Mixed Reality & Passthrough

Blending the real desk, keyboard and screen into virtual workspaces so people can work naturally inside XR.

Avatars & Embodiment

Body, hand and face tracking for believable avatars, and how embodiment changes the way people behave in shared spaces.

Shared Immersive Spaces

CAVE and nDisplay installations as collaborative environments, and how they connect with head-mounted and desktop users.

Inclusive & Accessible XR

Designing immersive technology and creative careers that are open to people of all abilities.
